Women Empowerment

Empowering Women to Transform Communities

When women are empowered, entire communities thrive. MASaD's Women Empowerment program focuses on providing women with the skills, resources, and opportunities they need to become leaders and change-makers in their communities.

Our Approach

We implement comprehensive programs to support women's economic and social empowerment:

  • Skills training and vocational programs
  • Microfinance and small business support
  • Leadership and capacity building workshops
  • Women's rights advocacy and awareness
  • Mentorship and networking opportunities
  • Support for women entrepreneurs

Impact

Our women empowerment initiatives have created lasting change:

  • Trained 40+ women in vocational skills
  • Supported 40+ women-owned businesses
