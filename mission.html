Our Mission

Empowering communities to overcome challenges and build brighter futures

To advocate for and empower women, youth, marginalized and minority communities through education, sustainable development, human rights, and inclusive social protection initiatives. MASaD promotes equal access to education, healthcare, justice, and economic opportunities while combating discrimination, exclusion, and all forms of social injustice, enabling every individual to participate fully in the development of their communities.

MASaD is a non-profit organization based in Baidoa, Somalia. We work side by side with local communities, authorities and partners, including UNTMIS, to deliver humanitarian assistance and sustainable development programs that last.

What Drives Us

  • Providing humanitarian assistance to people in need
  • Delivering sustainable development programs in education, health and livelihoods
  • Empowering women, youth and vulnerable groups
  • Strengthening community resilience and self-reliance
  • Building partnerships with local authorities and international organizations

Our Commitment

We are committed to working transparently and respectfully with every community we serve, so that people can overcome challenges and build brighter futures for themselves and their children.
