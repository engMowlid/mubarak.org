Resilience & Livehoods

Building Economic Independence

Economic empowerment is key to breaking the cycle of poverty. MASaD's Livelihood Support program creates sustainable economic opportunities for individuals and families through skills training, small business support, and financial inclusion initiatives.

Our Approach

We provide comprehensive support to help communities achieve economic independence:

  • Vocational and skills training programs
  • Small business startup grants and loans
  • Agricultural support and farming resources
  • Financial literacy and savings groups
  • Market access and business development
  • Cooperative formation and support
