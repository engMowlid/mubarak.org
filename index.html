Mubarak Association for Sustainable and Development (MASaD)

Is a non-profit organization dedicated to building resilient communities for sustainable development. We work to empower communities through sustainable development programs, advocacy, and humanitarian assistance in Somalia and beyond.

Who We Are

Mubarak Association for Sustainable Development (MASaD) is a non-profit, non-governmental organization established in Baidoa south west state of Somalia, on 17 October 2020 and committed to promoting sustainable development and building resilient communities in South West State of Somalia and beyond.

Our Mission

To provide humanitarian assistance and sustainable development programs that empower communities to overcome challenges and build brighter futures.
